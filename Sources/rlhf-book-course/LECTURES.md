## Companion Implementations

The original [code library README](source/code/README.md) documents the reference implementations and suggested experiments. The library is preserved for reading and optional experiments; no dependencies, models, or training data have been installed.

## Suggested Seminar Reading Order

Read Lectures 2–3 for reward models and RL, Lecture 5 for reasoning and RLVR, and Lecture 6 for DPO. Then use Lectures 9–10 for over-optimization and regularization, and Lecture 12 for evaluation. Read Q&A 2 alongside the equations because it records published corrections. The [summary](SUMMARY.md) connects these topics to DeepSeek-R1 and Snell et al.
